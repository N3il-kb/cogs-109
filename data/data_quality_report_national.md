**Checks pass:**
- `covid_*` are **exactly 0 for all years 2010–2019** ✓ and populated 2020–2023 ✓. Curve peaks in **2021** (161 deaths/100k) then falls — the expected pandemic shape.
- `wfh_rate` shows the COVID jump: ~4% flat through 2019, then 4.78 → 6.30 → 8.11 → 9.59 (2019→2022) ✓.
- **Caveat (important for the COVID defense):** ACS WFH is a **5-year rolling average**, so the 2020 estimate blends 2016–2020 and the pandemic spike is smeared across vintages — the true single-year 2021 WFH spike was far larger (~17–18% nationally in 1-yr ACS). The 5-yr series understates the jump but is the only series available for all 169 small counties. The monotonic rise is unambiguous.
- **covid 2024 = 0** reflects *no NYT data* (series ended), not a true zero. Treat 2024 covid as missing, not zero, in any 2024 analysis.

## Coverage gaps (which counties/years/sources, and why)

- **LAUS 2023–2024 (all 169):** BLS blocks direct download (403) and no Wayback snapshot of `laucnty23/24.txt` exists. Unemployment_rate/labor_force are NaN for those 2 years.
- **EIA 2023 (all 169):** EIA's `f8612023.zip` is a 67KB stub (early/partial release); the full 2024 file (4.5MB) parsed fine. Elec rate is NaN for 2023 only.
- **ACS 2024 (all 169):** 5-year ACS 2024 vintage not released as of June 2026. All ACS-derived columns (incl. wfh_rate) NaN for 2024.
- **7 VA counties (BEA):** Fairfax, Prince William, Augusta, James City, Carroll, Lynchburg city, Petersburg city report income/GDP only under BEA *combined* independent-city areas (e.g. 51919 = Fairfax+Fairfax City+Falls Church). Their per_capita_income/gdp_thousands are the **combined-area** values — a documented BEA limitation, not missing data.
- **~5 counties/year (EIA):** small or independent-city counties whose serving utilities don't cleanly map to a county get NaN elec rate (within the original script's ~3% expected miss).
- **No county exceeds 25% missingness** across the core outcomes (income, elec rate, employment, unemployment, median income) — **nothing needs dropping for data reasons.**
